## Mathematical viability versus evidential selection

Two questions must be kept distinct.

### Question A — viability

Can candidate \(C_i\) be instantiated as a mathematically coherent ternary
object?

### Question B — selection

Does the initiating evidence discriminate \(C_i\) from competing ternary
objects?

Phase 1-4 provide exceptionally strong evidence for the viability of C1.

They do not automatically provide evidence for selection of C1 over C0-C4.

## Genericity safeguard

A match receives less discriminatory weight when many unrelated mathematical
objects share the same feature.

Examples:

- having three inputs;
- being trilinear;
- being alternating;
- being invariant under some group.

A feature is more discriminating only if it was present in the initiating
evidence BEFORE candidate construction and is comparatively rare among the
candidate set.
