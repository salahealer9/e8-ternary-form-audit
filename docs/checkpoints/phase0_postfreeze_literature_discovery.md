## Material discovery

Manivel develops binary, ternary, and triality models of exceptional Lie
algebras.

For the exceptional series he gives a ternary construction of the form

\[
\mathfrak g(\mathbb A,\mathbb O)
=
\mathfrak{sl}_3
\times
\mathfrak g(\mathbb A,\mathbb C)
\oplus
(\mathbb C^3\otimes J_3(\mathbb A))
\oplus
(\mathbb C^3\otimes J_3(\mathbb A))^*.
\]

For the E8 case, this specializes to a decomposition of the form

\[
\mathfrak e_8
=
\mathfrak{sl}_3
\times
\mathfrak e_6
\oplus
(B\otimes J)
\oplus
(B\otimes J)^*.
\]

Manivel explicitly refers to this as the

    ternary model of e8

and states that there are

\[
1120
\]

associated azygetic triads of complexes.

The construction uses triples arising when two relevant non-orthogonal
root-labelled objects can be completed by a third associated with a root of
the form

\[
\gamma=\alpha\pm\beta.
\]

This is directly relevant to the preregistered investigation of:

- ternary E8 structure;
- A2 / sl3 structure;
- the number 1120;
- the E8 relationship with E6;
- triples generated from relationships among E8 roots.

## Prior-art consequence

The following must NOT be claimed as discoveries of this repository:

1. that E8 admits a mathematically natural "ternary model";
2. that an sl3 x e6 decomposition of e8 exists;
3. that E8 contains a distinguished family counted by 1120 in this
   ternary/A2-related setting;
4. that exceptional Lie-algebra models can naturally be organized through
   binary, ternary, and triality constructions.

These are established prior art.

## What remains open in this repository

questions.

   \[
   \alpha+\beta+\gamma=0;

   1120 A2 root subsystems;

4. a precisely defined root-level ternary incidence object, provisionally

   \[
   T_{ijk};
   \]

5. the relationship between that discrete support structure and the Cartan
   3-form

   \[
   \Omega(X,Y,Z)=B(X,[Y,Z]);

   initiating notation

   \[

   structure.

performed at this stage is not evidence of absence from the mathematical
literature.

2. a separate prior-art search targeted to that definition;
3. independent computational or algebraic verification;
4. explicit separation from established E8 root-system and Lie-algebra facts.
