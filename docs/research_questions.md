# Research Questions

## RQ0 — Minimal interpretation

Does the remembered expression

\[
g(v_1,v_2,v_3)
\]

require anything more mathematically specific than a generic three-argument
function?

Null position:

No. Three-argument function notation is mathematically ordinary.

## RQ1 — Canonical ternary structures

Are there established mathematical structures for which three vector-like
arguments are intrinsic rather than incidental?

Candidate examples include invariant 3-forms and symmetric trilinear forms.

## RQ2 — Direct E8 structure

Does the Lie algebra \(\mathfrak e_8\) possess a canonical invariant ternary
form?

Candidate:

\[
\Omega(X,Y,Z)=B(X,[Y,Z]).
\]

## RQ3 — Root-level support

When the Cartan 3-form is evaluated on root-space vectors, is its possible
nonzero support controlled by zero-sum root triples

\[
\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0?
\]

This will be derived algebraically and checked computationally.

## RQ4 — E8 ternary incidence

What finite combinatorial object is obtained by enumerating all unordered
triples of E8 roots satisfying

\[
\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0?
\]

Quantities to determine independently include:

- number of roots;
- number of eligible root pairs;
- number of ordered zero-sum triples;
- number of unordered zero-sum triples;
- number of A2 subsystems;
- incidence degree of each root;
- orbit structure under relevant symmetries where computationally feasible.

## RQ5 — Relation to A2

Does every zero-sum root triple generate an A2 root configuration?

Conversely, how many zero-sum triples correspond to one A2 root subsystem?

## RQ6 — Tensor / hypergraph representation

Can the root-triple relation be represented usefully as a ternary incidence
tensor

\[
T_{ijk}
\]

or a 3-uniform hypergraph without introducing arbitrary choices?

## RQ7 — Metric analogy

Is there any mathematically justified sense in which a ternary object can play
a role analogous to, derive, enrich, or coexist with an ordinary binary metric

\[
g(v_i,v_j)?
\]

A notational resemblance alone is insufficient.

## RQ8 — Exceptional comparison

How does the E8 Cartan 3-form compare structurally with:

- the G2 invariant 3-form;
- the E6 cubic / polarized trilinear invariant?

Comparison dimensions include:

- symmetry type;
- domain dimension;
- invariance group;
- dependence on a binary bilinear form;
- algebraic degree;
- geometrical interpretation.

## RQ9 — Novelty

After established structures are reproduced, is there any nontrivial
mathematical statement suggested by the ternary E8 incidence structure that is
not merely a restatement of known root-system or Lie-algebra facts?

Any such claim must receive a separate preregistration before exploratory
testing.
