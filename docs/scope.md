## Phase 0 status

STATUS: SCOPE_FROZEN

## Initiating observation

The sole initiating datum is the remembered notation

\[
g(v_1,v_2,v_3).
\]

It was reported after a dream.

For the purposes of this project, the observation contains only:

1. a lowercase symbol `g`;
2. three arguments;
3. argument labels remembered as `v_1`, `v_2`, `v_3`;
4. the visual impression that `g` was italic.

No additional semantics are admitted into the initiating datum.

In particular, the observation does not establish that:

- `g` is a metric;
- `g` is multilinear;
- the `v_i` are vectors;
- the object is symmetric or antisymmetric;
- the object belongs to Lie theory;
- the object belongs to E8;
- the dream had an external or non-ordinary causal origin.

Italic mathematical variables are standard mathematical typography and are
therefore assigned no evidential weight.

## Mathematical scope

The investigation concerns established mathematical objects taking three
arguments, especially three vector or Lie-algebra arguments.

The initial exceptional-geometry candidate classes are:

### C0 — Generic function

\[
g: X^3 \to Y.
\]

This is the minimal interpretation and acts as the null structural hypothesis.

### C1 — Cartan 3-form

For a Lie algebra \(\mathfrak g\) with an invariant nondegenerate bilinear
form \(B\),

\[
\Omega(X,Y,Z)=B(X,[Y,Z]).
\]

Primary specialization:

\[
\mathfrak g=\mathfrak e_8.
\]

### C2 — G2 invariant 3-form

An alternating 3-form associated with the exceptional group \(G_2\).

### C3 — E6 cubic structure

A cubic invariant and its polarized symmetric trilinear form associated with
the 27-dimensional exceptional representation.

### C4 — Other established ternary structures

Other mathematically established cubic forms, 3-forms, higher-order metrics,
or ternary geometric constructions may be added only with explicit provenance.

## Out of scope at Phase 0

The following are not claims tested by the mathematical computation:

- whether dreams can transmit information;
- whether the dream was paranormal;
- whether the notation came from an external intelligence;
- whether E8 is a theory of consciousness;
- whether a discovered mathematical correspondence has physical significance.

Those questions require evidence outside the mathematical object studied here.
