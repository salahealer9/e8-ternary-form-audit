# Phase 0 Preregistration

## Status

STATUS: PHASE0_PREREGISTRATION_FROZEN

This document must be frozen before execution of the E8 root enumeration.

## P0. Initiating observation

The sole initiating observation is

\[
g(v_1,v_2,v_3).
\]

No mathematical meaning is assumed.

The dream context may motivate the investigation but is not mathematical
evidence for any candidate interpretation.

## P1. Primary objective

Determine whether the three-argument vector-like notation has a close,
non-arbitrary correspondence with established ternary structures in
exceptional mathematics, with the first rigorous case study being the Cartan
3-form of \(\mathfrak e_8\).

## P2. Candidate classes frozen before computation

- C0: generic three-argument function;
- C1: E8 Cartan 3-form;
- C2: G2 invariant alternating 3-form;
- C3: E6 cubic / symmetric trilinear form;
- C4: other established ternary structure with explicit provenance.

Candidates discovered later must be labelled POST_HOC_CANDIDATE and cannot
replace the preregistered candidates retrospectively.

## P3. Comparison criteria

Each candidate will eventually be assessed on independently defined
properties.

### K1 — Arity match

Does the object intrinsically take three arguments?

### K2 — Vector-like domain match

Are its arguments naturally vectors, tangent vectors, representation vectors,
or Lie-algebra elements?

### K3 — Canonicality

Is the object canonically or naturally defined once the relevant mathematical
structure is specified?

### K4 — Invariance

Is the ternary object preserved by a nontrivial symmetry group?

### K5 — Geometric content

Does it encode geometry rather than being an arbitrary user-defined function?

### K6 — Metric relation

Is there a precise relation to an ordinary bilinear metric or invariant
bilinear form?

### K7 — Direct E8 relevance

Is the object intrinsically associated with E8 rather than connected through
a discretionary chain of analogies?

No criterion may be reweighted after numerical results are known without
creating a new exploratory analysis.

## P4. Primary computational test

Construct the standard E8 root system from its defining coordinate rules.

The implementation must derive its root count from the construction.

It must then determine, without using benchmark counts as control parameters:

1. all roots;
2. all pairwise inner products;
3. all pairs satisfying \(\alpha\cdot\beta=-1\);
4. all triples satisfying \(\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0\);
5. all distinct A2 root subsystems generated by those triples.

## P5. Frozen external benchmarks

The following literature values are recorded before implementation:

\]

\[

\[

\[

These are replication targets only.

No program is permitted to construct its result by assuming these counts.

## P6. Derived expectations

The following arithmetic consequences are recorded before execution but must
also emerge from enumeration.

If all 240 roots each have 56 partners at inner product -1, the number of
ordered qualifying root pairs is expected to be

\[
240\times56=13440.
\]

An A2 root subsystem contains six roots and 12 ordered pairs with inner product
-1, suggesting

\[
13440/12=1120
\]

A2 subsystems.

For a zero-sum root triple,

\[
\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0,
\]

with all three roots of squared norm 2,

\[
\gamma=-(\alpha+\beta)
\]

implies

\[
2
=
2+2+2\alpha\cdot\beta,
\]

hence

\[
\alpha\cdot\beta=-1.
\]

The corresponding relations hold cyclically.

These derivations are checks, not enumeration instructions.

## P7. Independence rules

The primary root enumeration must not:

- stop when 240 roots have been found;
- sample until 56 neighbours have been found;
- stop when 1120 A2 subsystems have been found;
- insert expected triples from a precomputed external table.

The root-generation rules must be sufficient by themselves.

At least one later phase will implement an independent validation path.

## P8. Interpretation classes

Results will be classified conservatively.

### I0 — Generic notation only

No meaningful correspondence beyond the fact that many functions can have
three arguments.

### I1 — Established ternary analogue

One or more known canonical mathematical structures provide a genuine
three-vector analogue.

### I2 — Direct E8 correspondence

A canonical E8 ternary structure exists and its root-level support is
reproduced computationally.

### I3 — Additional structural result

The investigation reveals a mathematically nontrivial relationship not already
contained in the standard definitions or obvious root-system identities.

I3 requires separate verification and literature checking.

None of I0-I3 constitutes evidence about the causal origin of the dream.

## P9. Anti-pattern safeguards

The investigation will not count as evidence:

- arbitrary numerology;
- visual similarity alone;
- notation similarity alone;
- selective reporting of matching cases;
- changing definitions after seeing results;
- importing benchmark values into the computation;
- treating a known mathematical fact as a new discovery;
- treating mathematical correspondence as evidence of paranormal causation.
