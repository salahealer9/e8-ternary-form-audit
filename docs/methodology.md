# Methodology

## Purpose

This repository develops and validates an exact sparse realization of the
normalized \(E_8\) Cartan 3-form

\[
g(X,Y,Z)=B_0(X,[Y,Z])
\]

in a fixed \(\epsilon\)-canonical Chevalley basis.

The workflow was designed to separate:

1. preregistered questions and conventions;
2. exact mathematical construction;
3. independent computational validation;
4. interpretation and prior-art assessment;
5. manuscript and public-release preparation.

The initiating notation

\[
g(v_1,v_2,v_3)
\]

motivated the investigation but was not assigned mathematical meaning in
advance. The dream context in which it was remembered is treated only as
hypothesis-generating provenance and not as mathematical evidence.

## Exact arithmetic

All decisive support and equality tests use exact arithmetic.

The implementation uses:

- integral doubled coordinates for the \(E_8\) roots;
- integer Chevalley structure constants;
- integer sparse Cartan 3-form coefficients;
- rational arithmetic where arbitrary linear combinations require it.

No floating-point tolerance is used to decide whether a root, bracket,
tensor coefficient, Jacobi identity, or reconstruction equality vanishes.

## Phase structure

### Phase 0 — scope and preregistration

The project first froze:

- the initiating observation;
- candidate mathematical interpretations;
- comparison criteria;
- notation;
- benchmark values from external literature;
- rules preventing benchmark values from controlling the computation.

The benchmark values were treated as validation targets rather than
enumeration inputs.

### Phase 1 — \(E_8\) root construction

The \(240\)-root system was generated from its coordinate definition and
validated independently.

Checks included:

- root count;
- squared norm;
- uniqueness;
- rank;
- crystallographic integrality;
- reflection closure;
- root inner-product census.

### Phase 2 — ternary root incidence

All zero-sum root triples

\[
\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0
\]

were enumerated exactly.

The resulting finite structure contains:

\[
13{,}440
\]

ordered root triples,

\[
2240
\]

unordered zero-sum triples, and

\[
1120
\]

negation/\(A_2\) classes.

The unsigned root support is represented as a linear \(3\)-uniform
hypergraph on \(240\) vertices with degree \(28\).

### Phase 3 — Cartan 3-form construction

The root-incidence structure was lifted to the Lie-algebra level.

The basis consists of:

- \(8\) Cartan generators;
- \(240\) root vectors.

A fixed \(\epsilon\)-canonical Chevalley convention determines the root-vector
signs, and the invariant form is normalized by

\[
B_0(h_i,h_j)=a_{ij}.
\]

The complete sparse tensor contains:

\[
13{,}440
\]

root--root--root entries and

\[
2{,}736
\]

Cartan--root--root entries, for

\[
16{,}176
\]

nonzero ordered coefficients in total.

The coefficient census is

\[
-2:24,\qquad
-1:8064,\qquad
+1:8064,\qquad
+2:24.
\]

### Phase 4 — independent Lie-algebra audit

The Lie bracket and invariant form were reconstructed independently of the
sealed sparse tensor.

The validation includes:

- bracket closure;
- antisymmetry;
- zero self-brackets;
- exhaustive Jacobi verification;
- invariant-form symmetry and nondegeneracy;
- exact invariance of \(B_0\);
- reconstruction of the Cartan 3-form from the independently validated algebra.

The Jacobi identity was checked on all

\[
\binom{248}{3}=2{,}511{,}496
\]

distinct basis triples.

### Phase 5 — interpretation synthesis

The candidate interpretations were compared against the frozen criteria.

The \(E_8\) Cartan 3-form was identified as a direct mathematical realization
of a canonical ternary structure associated with \(E_8\).

This does not imply that the remembered notation uniquely denotes that
structure or that its dream context has any external causal significance.

### Phase 6 — novelty and literal realization

The project then:

- audited prior art;
- distinguished classical ingredients from the computational contribution;
- implemented the literal callable
  \[
  g(v_1,v_2,v_3)=B_0(v_1,[v_2,v_3]);
  \]
- verified exact bracket recovery from \(g\) and \(B_0\).

The novelty claim is deliberately limited to the explicit sparse realization,
coefficient census, root-incidence packaging, deterministic data artifact,
and reproducibility architecture.

No new \(E_8\) invariant is claimed.

## Two tensor construction routes

The final sparse tensor is generated by two algorithmically distinct exact
routes.

### Route A

Construct coefficients directly from ordered Lie brackets and invariant
pairings.

### Route B

Start from canonical unordered zero-sum root triples and opposite-root lines,
evaluate one orientation, and recover all permutations by alternation.

The two resulting sparse maps agree entry-for-entry.

## Independent validation principle

The principal validation path does not define the Lie algebra from the sealed
tensor.

Instead:

1. the bracket is reconstructed independently;
2. the invariant form is reconstructed independently;
3. the Lie algebra is exhaustively validated;
4. \(B_0(X,[Y,Z])\) is recomputed;
5. the result is compared against the already frozen sparse tensor.

This separation is intended to reduce circular validation.

## Scientific boundary

The mathematical results are intended to stand independently of the
project's initiating context.

The repository does not treat as scientific evidence:

- notation similarity alone;
- dream provenance;
- visual analogy;
- numerology;
- post hoc redefinition of criteria;
- known mathematical facts relabeled as discoveries.

The manuscript therefore presents the final \(E_8\) construction as a
mathematical and computational result built from classical Lie-theoretic
structures.
